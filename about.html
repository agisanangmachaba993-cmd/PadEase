About PadEase

Making period care affordable, comfortable and accessible.

Who We Are

PadEase is a menstrual hygiene brand focused on making quality sanitary products more affordable and accessible to girls and women.

We believe that every woman should have access to reliable period products without having to worry about affordability. PadEase provides convenient and affordable period-care solutions for everyday needs.

Our Purpose

Affordable

We aim to provide period products at affordable prices for our customers.

Reliable

Our products are designed to provide dependable period protection and comfort.

Accessible

We want menstrual hygiene products to be easier for girls and women to access.
